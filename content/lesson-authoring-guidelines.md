# Learner-question guidelines

Use realistic daily-life or workplace situations for grammar and final-test questions. Do not ask learners to identify a grammar label or rule as the task itself.

Each question should:

- present a natural message, conversation, direction, or work task;
- have one clearly best answer, with realistic distractors;
- assess grammar through meaning and real use rather than terminology; and
- include a short, plain-English explanation shown only after a correct answer when a grammar choice is being practised.

For future lessons, use the same approach in grammar practice and final tests. Reserve explicit rule labels for the grammar guide, not the question prompt.
